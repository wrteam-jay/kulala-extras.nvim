1. Introduction                                     *kulala-extras-introduction*

kulala-extras.nvim adds response history and a diff-based compare view on
top of kulala.nvim (https://github.com/mistweaverco/kulala.nvim), using its
public `require("kulala.api").on("after_request", ...)` hook.

==============================================================================
2. Setup                                                   *kulala-extras-setup*

>lua
    require("kulala-extras").setup({
      -- history_dir = vim.fn.stdpath("data") .. "/kulala-extras/history",
      -- max_history_per_request = 50,
    })
<

==============================================================================
3. Commands                                             *kulala-extras-commands*

                                                *:KulalaExtrasCompareHere*
:KulalaExtrasCompareHere    Diff the two most recent responses for the
                             request under the cursor. One step, no
                             picker - the common case.

                                                   *:KulalaExtrasOpenHere*
:KulalaExtrasOpenHere       Pick one past response for the request under
                             the cursor, open it in a side split.

                                                    *:KulalaExtrasCompare*
:KulalaExtrasCompare        Full picker: pick a request, then two past
                             responses, and diff them in a native Neovim
                             diff split.

                                                *:KulalaExtrasDebugPayload*
:KulalaExtrasDebugPayload   One-shot: print the raw `after_request`
                             payload of the next response you run.
